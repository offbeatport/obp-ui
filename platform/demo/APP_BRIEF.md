# App Brief: Demo

## Tagline

Generate 5 compelling taglines for any product - instantly, for free.

## Archetype

tool-first

## Style

Cobalt

## Radius

Subtle

## Audience

Indie hackers, founders, and marketers who need to nail product positioning copy fast.

## Main Job

Turn a product description into 5 memorable taglines in under 5 seconds.

## V1 Features

- Input: product name + one-line description
- Output: 5 AI-generated taglines, each under 10 words
- Copy any tagline to clipboard
- Rate limited: 3 free generations per day (anon), 10 for signed-in users

## Retention Loop

Users return when they have a new product, update positioning, or share with teammates.

## Monetization Moment

Upgrade CTA when daily anon limit (3) is hit - "Get 10/day free with an account".

## Success Signal

50+ organic generations/day within 30 days of launch.

## Out of Scope (V1)

- Saved history
- Custom brand voice
- Multi-language output
- Batch generation

## Risks

- OpenRouter key cost if viral - mitigated by rate limiting
- Low differentiation - mitigated by being the canonical factory demo

## Distribution

- First channel: pain-thread reply (linked from relevant "how do I write a tagline" posts)
- Second channel: HN Show HN post when it's polished

- origin_idea_id: (local - not from a cluster)
- origin_pain_signal_id: (none)
