The core loop is live and working: a customer embeds our webform, every submission is captured, and each lead is auto-scored 0–100 by buying intent so the sales-ready ones rise to the top. Twelve accounts rely on it daily, none have left, and the first paid tier is holding at $15/seat. Costs are trivial, so nearly every new dollar is margin.
