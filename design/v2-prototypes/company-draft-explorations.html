Company spec · full
NudgePay
In one line

Freelancers get paid on time without the awkward chase. Grown from the Auto-Nudge opportunity (#1).

Who it’s for

Solo freelancers who bill via Stripe or Wave and routinely get paid late - reachable in freelancer subreddits & Discords. The wedge: people will pay to never write the awkward “any update on that invoice?” email again - not for another invoicing tool.

Pricing & packaging
Price
$12 / mo · solo
Trial
14-day free
Mode
Test-mode

Stripe runs in test-mode while it’s built - no real charges. Once it’s live and proven, the operator gets a “Take it live” action to switch on real payments.

V1 scope · 5 slices
1
Connect Stripe + day-7 reminder
SANITY-CHECK - the operator’s one manual check.
doneWhen: a test overdue invoice fires an email
2
Escalating reminder ladder
7 / 14 / 21-day cadence; the tone steps up politely.
doneWhen: three reminders queue at the right offsets
3
Client pay-status page
A branded link showing what’s owed and overdue.
doneWhen: a public link renders the invoice state
4
“Mark paid” + stop reminders
One click halts the ladder for that invoice.
doneWhen: marking paid cancels pending reminders
5
Weekly “who owes you” digest
A Monday summary of outstanding invoices.
doneWhen: a digest email lists the overdue invoices
Out of scope for V1
  • Real-money charging (test-mode only until proven)
  • Multi-currency & tax handling
  • Team seats / agency accounts
  • Accounting export & a mobile app
Tech stack
  • Next.js - the app + hosted pay-status pages
  • Stripe API - invoices + test-mode payments
  • Resend - transactional email sent from the user’s own domain
  • Postgres / Supabase - data + auth
How we’ll know it’s working
  • Activation - first reminder sent within 48h of signup
  • Core metric - median days-to-paid drops vs the user’s baseline (target: 23 → <12)
  • Retention proxy - the weekly “who owes you” digest gets opened
