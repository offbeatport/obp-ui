LeadSift

Sales · built by @maya

Most inbound tools bury you in noise. LeadSift does the opposite — it reads every webform lead the second it lands and hands you a single number, 0 to 100, so you know exactly who to call first. No dashboards to configure, no rules to write. Just: this one's hot, ring them now.

"The rare AI product that makes your day smaller, not busier — a shortlist instead of a firehose."

Maya shipped a Daily digest two hours ago and the numbers are quietly compounding: twelve paying teams, a healthy $180 a month, and a signal our editors would call Strong. It is exactly the kind of small, sharp, revenue-first company this platform was built to make.
