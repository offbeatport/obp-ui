The heart of it
The 5 gates - where you touch the loop
Ship - approve a build slice make
Spend - authorize money out: ads, domains, infra grow / run
Post - publish under your identity: launch, social, outreach, email grow
Sign - legal: entity, ToS, contracts run
Unblock - resolve a stuck slice or decision any ring
Why it can / can't auto-run
Authorization boundary - 5 action classes
Pure software - runs silently, fully autonomous
Connected account - acts via your OAuth'd services: Stripe, registrar, ESP, social, ads, host
Spends money - needs budget guardrail + gate
Acts under your identity - reputation / ban risk → test-mode → record-intent → real
Needs a signature - human / legal authority
